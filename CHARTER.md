# Oblivion-AI Global Public-Sector Charter

## Mission

Oblivion-AI exists to help public institutions authorize consequential AI-assisted actions before execution and produce independently verifiable evidence afterward. It is designed for jurisdictional adaptation, sovereign operation, democratic accountability, and meaningful human remedy. It is not a decision maker, regulator, legal authority, or substitute for accountable officials.

## Product direction

The platform's target capability is **A2SPA-R: Sovereign AI Action Receipts**. An authorized action should bind the actor or workload, purpose, legal/policy authority, approved data use, model and tool provenance, policy version, jurisdiction, human oversight, time, nonce, and retention class. The resulting receipt should disclose only the evidence needed by an operator, auditor, oversight body, court, or affected person while retaining integrity and revocation status.

## Global principles

1. **Local authority:** Every deployment maps local law, policy, language, records, accessibility, procurement, security, and constitutional obligations into versioned policy packs approved by the deploying institution.
2. **Sovereign control:** Institutions select and evidence data, key, model, telemetry, backup, support, and disaster-recovery locations. No cloud or country is mandatory.
3. **No authority by automation:** A model recommendation cannot create legal authority. High-impact actions require an identified accountable official and review proportionate to risk.
4. **Purpose limitation:** Authorization is bound to a declared purpose, data scope, recipient, expiry, and permitted action.
5. **Human remedy:** Affected people receive intelligible notice where lawful, and accessible routes for correction, objection, review, appeal, and effective human remedy.
6. **Evidence over claims:** Compliance, security, fairness, and production-readiness claims require scoped, current, independently reviewable evidence.
7. **Inclusive operation:** Interfaces and notices support localization, plain language, assistive technology, low-bandwidth access, and non-digital alternatives.
8. **Interoperability:** Contracts and receipts use versioned, portable formats and avoid mandatory dependence on one vendor.

## Prohibited uses

The platform must not be configured to conceal accountable actors, bypass due process, enable unlawful surveillance or discrimination, infer authority from a model output, suppress appeal, or execute a consequential action without required authorization. Deployments involving coercion, liberty, essential services, biometrics, children, migration, policing, or benefits require enhanced legal, rights, security, and independent review.

## Adoption gate

Before live use, each institution must publish or internally approve a deployment charter identifying the controller and service owner, jurisdiction and applicable obligations, permitted and prohibited purposes, affected groups, appeal path, oversight body, policy pack, data locations, retention, incident process, independent assessors, and shutdown authority. This repository does not satisfy that gate by itself.
