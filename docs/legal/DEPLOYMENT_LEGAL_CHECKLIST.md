# Legal Publication Checklist

The public legal pages are repository baselines, not a substitute for legal advice. Before exposing any BeyondBeams instance to real users or data, the accountable operator and qualified counsel must complete and approve all applicable items.

- Legal entity name, registration details, physical address, and public contact
- Service owner, controller, joint-controller, and processor roles
- Privacy contact, data protection officer where required, and supervisory authority
- Governing law, courts or dispute mechanism, and mandatory public-sector terms
- Exact service scope, user eligibility, account authority, support, and accessibility routes
- Purposes, data categories, affected groups, lawful bases, and prohibited uses
- Processors, subprocessors, recipients, hosting and backup locations, and transfer safeguards
- Retention schedule, deletion, legal hold, records access, and decommissioning
- Cookies and browser storage verified against the deployed build and identity provider
- Rights request, notice, correction, objection, human review, appeal, complaint, and remedy routes
- Security, incident notification, breach reporting, and vulnerability-reporting contacts
- Intellectual-property ownership, third-party notices, procurement terms, warranty, liability, indemnity, and insurance positions
- Children, vulnerable groups, biometrics, essential services, and other high-risk processing restrictions
- Localization, plain-language, accessible-format, and non-digital publication review
- Version owner, approval date, review cadence, archived prior versions, and change-notice process

Approval of these documents does not by itself establish compliance or production readiness. The evidence gates in `PRODUCTION_ASSURANCE.md`, `PRODUCTION_ROADMAP.md`, and `PROJECT_STATUS.md` remain open.
