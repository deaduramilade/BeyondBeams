# BeyondBeams Privacy Notice

**Effective:** 2026-08-14  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Development-project baseline; not approved for production

## Important deployment notice

This notice describes the current BeyondBeams repository and development interface. It is not jurisdiction-specific legal advice and does not identify a production controller. Before public or production use, the deploying institution or company must replace or approve this notice with its legal name, registered address, jurisdiction, privacy contact, data protection officer where required, controller and processor roles, supervisory authority, and service-specific processing details.

This repository does not authorize use with real personal data.

## Information handled

Public pages can be viewed without an account. When institutional sign-in is configured, an instance may process:

- identity, role, scope, tenant, workload, and authorization claims;
- opaque session and anti-forgery data;
- case inputs, workflow history, notes, and evidence metadata;
- policy decisions, signed authorization envelopes, and receipts;
- audit records and operational security events; and
- technical request information handled by the selected hosting and identity providers.

Case content may contain personal or sensitive information depending on the operator's configuration. Development and synthetic evaluation must not include real personal data.

## Purposes and lawful basis

Information is intended to authenticate authorized users, enforce tenant and policy controls, manage governed cases, support human review and remedy, preserve security and audit evidence, prevent misuse, and operate the service. The deploying operator must identify and document the lawful basis for every purpose and category of information. Consent is not a universal or default basis.

## Sharing, processors, and transfers

The repository does not select production hosting, identity, telemetry, storage, notification, scanning, or support providers. The operator must disclose actual processors and recipients, data and backup locations, international transfers and safeguards, government-access rules, and procurement controls. The project does not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

## Retention and security

Development adapters use local, single-host storage and do not provide production custody or durability. The operator must define and test record-specific retention, deletion, legal hold, backup, access, encryption, key custody, incident response, and recovery controls. No system can be represented as absolutely secure.

## Individual rights and automated decisions

Depending on applicable law and the operator's role, people may have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, complaint, and protection concerning automated decisions. Requests must be directed to the deploying operator through its published privacy and non-digital channels.

BeyondBeams outputs are simulated recommendations or records. They are not legal determinations and must not be treated as authority for solely automated consequential decisions. Deployments must preserve applicable notice, human review, correction, objection, appeal, and remedy routes.

## Children

The development service is not directed to children. It must not be configured for children's data without enhanced legal, safeguarding, rights, security, and independent review.

## Changes

Material changes to purposes, data, providers, jurisdictions, or architecture require this notice and the applicable assessments to be reviewed. The public application version is hosted at `/legal/privacy`.
