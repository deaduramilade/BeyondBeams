# BEYONDBEAMS Brand Guide

## Core identity

- Name: `BEYONDBEAMS`
- Primary logo: `dashboard/logo.svg`
- Logo colour: pure black (`#000000`) on a transparent background
- Favicon and compact logo: `dashboard/favicon.svg`, a segmented `BB` monogram
- Hero banner: `dashboard/beyondbeams-banner.svg` (1920 × 600)

## Typography

- Branding, headings, navigation, buttons and labels: Michroma, regular weight. The wordmark uses wide tracking, angled stencil cuts through each letter, and segmented technical rules.
- Paragraphs and long-form content: Inter, regular weight.

The dashboard loads both fonts from Google Fonts. Use the provided fallbacks only when the font service is unavailable.

## Visual language

The BeyondBeams system is premium, high-contrast, minimal and futuristic. Use black structural forms with a gold palette (`#f2c34b`, `#ffdd76`, `#6d4611`). Banner layouts pair metallic gold gradients with fine technical lines, sparse dot matrices and angular black corner architecture. The brand name and compact monogram always use the geometric techno-stencil treatment. Keep clear space around the logo and preserve its pure-black colour on light or gold surfaces.

## Responsive use

The banner uses an SVG viewBox and CSS `object-fit: cover`, preserving the visual at desktop and mobile widths. Do not rasterize or stretch the SVG logo.
