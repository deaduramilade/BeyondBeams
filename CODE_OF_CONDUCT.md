# Code of Conduct

## Standard

Participants must communicate professionally, treat others with respect, welcome good-faith technical disagreement, protect confidential and personal information, and focus criticism on work and evidence. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and retaliation are unacceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, reviews, issues, project communication channels, events, and public representation of the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to project maintainers through an approved private channel; do not disclose sensitive reports publicly. Maintainers must avoid conflicts of interest, preserve confidentiality, gather relevant facts, allow a fair response where safe, and document proportionate action. Actions may include guidance, warning, temporary restriction, removal, or referral to the relevant platform or authority.

Retaliation against reporters or participants in an investigation is prohibited. False reports made knowingly and maliciously are also violations; an unsubstantiated good-faith report is not.
